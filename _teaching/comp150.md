**Loyola University Chicago — Spring 2026**

This course has concluded. Materials are kept here for reference.

**Note on dates:** Many of the slide decks below have "Spring 2025" printed on their
title slides. That date is incorrect — this course ran in Spring 2026. The files are
otherwise unmodified.

A programming course for beginners, covering the basics of designing, writing, and
running programs in [Python](https://www.python.org/). No prior programming experience
required.
